TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN

THE MAIN STRAIGHT & RETTIFILO

The lap begins on one of the longest straights in Formula 1, with cars reaching speeds exceeding 350 km/h before aggressively slamming on the brakes for Turn 1 (Prima Variante). This chicane requires perfect car placement and provides the best overtaking opportunity on the track.

  • Turns 1 & 2: Variante del Rettifilo
  • Speed Trap: 352 km/h (End of straight)

CURVA GRANDE & LESMOS

Sector 2 flows through the high-speed Curva Grande (Turn 3) into the second chicane, Variante della Roggia. Drivers then face the two Lesmo corners—medium-high speed right-handers that demand absolute precision. A slight misjudgment here carries massive speed penalties down the following straight.

  • Turn 3: Curva Grande
  • Turns 4 & 5: Variante della Roggia
  • Turns 6 & 7: Lesmo 1 & 2

ASCARI & PARABOLICA

The final sector is defined by two iconic complexes. The Variante Ascari is a rapid left-right-left chicane that requires massive commitment over the curbs. The lap finishes with the legendary Curva Alboreto (formerly Parabolica), a long, sweeping right-hander that slingshots the cars back onto the main straight.

  • Turns 8, 9, 10: Variante Ascari
  • Turn 11: Curva Alboreto (Parabolica)
